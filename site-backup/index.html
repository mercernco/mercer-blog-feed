01 · Who we are

Who we are.

Mercer & Co. is a New York distribution company. We are the authorized distributor of Vesta Kitchen for New York. We bring the brand to local shops, so dealers can offer their customers more choice without dealing with cross border ordering.

The brand promise

Fresh Air,
Fresh Living.

Founded in Toronto, Canada in 2010. An NKBA member company.

The brand story.

Vesta Kitchen was founded in 2010 in Toronto, Canada. The company started with one focus: range hoods built for real family cooking.

Over the years it grew into a full home appliance brand, known for practical design, strong build quality, and fair pricing.

Its promise is simple: Fresh Air, Fresh Living. Vesta Kitchen is a member company of the National Kitchen and Bath Association (NKBA).

New York customers deserve more choice. For years, shoppers here could only pick from the same few big brands.

Vesta gives your customers a new option: powerful ventilation, modern design, and honest pricing, from an entry level line to a pro grade line.
